=== US4 evidence: automatic daily backup, triggered by the scheduled mechanism itself ===

IMPORTANT: this backup was NOT triggered by running `scripts/backup.sh` by
hand. The permanent daily schedule lives in `scripts/backup-schedule.env`
(default 02:30 UTC, outside simulated usage hours, FR-008) and is polled
every ~30s by `scripts/backup-cron-loop.sh`, running as the entrypoint of
the `backup-cron` container (docker-compose.yml). To demonstrate the
mechanism without waiting a full day, `backup-schedule.env` was edited to
set BACKUP_HOUR=18 / BACKUP_MINUTE=25 (about 2 minutes ahead of real time)
and then reset back to 02:30 UTC immediately after capturing this evidence.
The `backup-cron` container itself detected the matching clock time and
invoked `scripts/backup.sh` -- no different/manual command was run.
